## Foundation rework (started 2026-10-10, alpha: no backward compatibility)

The user found that a cut of four circles cut only one, that edges and sketch lines could not be picked (so a revolve axis or a fillet edge could not be chosen), and asked for the base to be fixed. Plan, in the order it is being done:

1. [x] **Features take lists, not single things.** `profiles: Vec<String>` replaces `profile` (empty = every closed region); the feature page has a checklist and clicking regions in the view adds or removes them. A revolve needs exactly one and says so. Done 2026-10-10.
2. [x] **One picker, one selection model.** `dc_render::pick_hit` returns a face, an edge (named by its two faces, `a|b`) or a sketch line, filtered like SolidWorks' selection filter (`select.faces/edges/sketch_lines` commands) and narrowed by the command being set up. `Selected` gained `Edge` and `SketchEntity`; hover, selection and chosen edges are drawn over the view. Fillet and chamfer take edges by clicking them; a revolve takes a sketch line as its axis. Delete with a face or edge selected no longer deletes the feature. Done 2026-10-10.
3. [x] **Rounding the circular rim of a shaft or a hole** (cone and torus surfaces, exact area and volume, tessellation, validation). Chamfer and fillet of the rim between a flat end and a full cylinder wall, outer rims and hole mouths, either end. Checked against frusta and Pappus. The rounded rims are kept on the layered model and redone after every later cut or boss, so cuts and bosses (for example from the flat top of a filleted shaft) still work; a cut that reaches into the rounded zone is refused with the reason. Done 2026-10-10.
4. [ ] **Reference geometry as document data:** offset planes, axes (through two points, along a cylinder or an edge), points. Features and sketches should be able to point at them.
5. [ ] **Stable names for edges** beyond the `face|face` pair (two edges between the same two faces are rounded together today).
6. [ ] **Replace the layered (stepped) model with a general B-rep boolean** and more surface types (sphere, general cone, NURBS): holes in side faces, angled cuts, partial revolves, sweep, loft, shell, rounding across any edge, and cuts/bosses on rounded parts. This is the largest item and the main limit of the engine; it needs surface-surface intersection (ellipses, quartics) and robust classification, and should start with plane/cylinder only.
7. [ ] Selection: Ctrl-click multi-select and box select in 3D; click selects the feature and a second click drills to the face; cuts from a face should go into the material by default.
